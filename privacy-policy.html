Vandal — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Vandal is a browser extension that lets you draw and annotate web pages. This policy describes what happens with your information when you use it.

Data we collect

We do not run our own servers for Vandal and we do not ask you to create an account. The extension does not send your browsing activity, page content, or annotations to us.

Data stored on your device

Vandal saves settings and UI preferences (for example toolbar layout and tool options) using your browser’s local storage. That data stays on your computer and is only used to make the extension work the way you left it.

Screenshots

When you capture the visible tab, the image is created in your browser. What you do with that file (save, share, etc.) is up to you and your browser; we do not receive a copy.

Third parties

Vandal does not use advertising or analytics SDKs. If you open a link from the extension (for example to a website), that site’s own privacy policy applies there.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the publisher listed with the extension in the store or repository.
